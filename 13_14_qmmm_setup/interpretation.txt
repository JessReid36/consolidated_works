# 13_14_qmmm_setup — QM/MM bridge, smoke test and DFT single point

Covers pipeline steps 13a, 13b and 14 as one unit: they establish the QM/MM partition
that every barrier in Phase 1 depends on, and validate it.

## What this step did
step13a converts the AMBER topology into an ORCAFF force-field bridge with ORCA 6.0.1
orca_mm -convff -AMBER. step13b runs a cheap HF-3c QM/MM single point as a smoke test.
step14 runs the production-level DFT single point that fixes the level of theory.

## Key numbers
- QM region: atoms 6207:6230 in ORCA's 0-based convention = 6208:6231 in the 1-based
  prmtop = CHA#2, the 24-atom substrate, and nothing else.
- Charge -2, multiplicity 1, confirmed both in the input and in ORCA's own echo.
- Link atoms: ORCA reports "Number of link atoms ... 0" and "Size of QM Subsystem plus
  link atoms ... 24". The QM region is a whole molecule, so no bond is cut.
- Bridge: complex_solvated.ORCAFF.prms, 8.6 MB.
- Level of theory: B3LYP D3BJ def2-SVP def2/J RIJCOSX, electrostatic embedding.

## THE ENERGY-SCALE IDENTITY, AND WHY IT MATTERS
The DFT single point reports three energies:

    QM (embedded substrate)   -836.786437201 Eh
    MM (environment)          -236.665890454 Eh
    QM/MM total              -1073.452327655 Eh

QM/MM = QM + MM exactly, residual 1.14e-12 Eh.

These are DIFFERENT SCALES. The project's own write-up prompt lists confusing them as a
known trap, and the harvest header repeats the warning. What the identity shows is why:
the embedded QM energy already contains the polarisation of the substrate by the
environment, and the MM term is the environment's own energy. Differencing a QM-only
bare-substrate energy near -836 Eh against a QM/MM total near -1073 Eh is subtracting the
entire protein and solvent, not an interaction.

This is exactly what E_int_R and E_int_P in ensemble_barriers.tsv measure, and the harvest
header already says so: "EMBEDDED QM energy minus gas-phase energy at the same geometry.
Interaction plus polarisation, NOT a stabilisation. Never difference against E_ci_Eh or
any other QM/MM total."

## Literature comparison
THE QM/MM PARTITION MATCHES CLAEYSSENS EXACTLY. Chorismate-only QM region, electrostatic
embedding, no link atoms. That is the correct design decision and it reproduces the
primary comparator's setup.

THE LEVEL OF THEORY DOES NOT. Claeyssens use B3LYP/6-31G(d)/CHARMM27; ours is
B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVP on ff14SB. Two separate changes, recorded as deviation 9:

  - D3BJ ADDED. Claeyssens explicitly name dispersion as a limitation of their own
    calculation (p. 10): "dispersion is not represented, which can be important for some
    enzyme-catalysed reactions". Adding it addresses a deficiency the comparator itself
    identifies. Present it that way, with their sentence cited.
  - def2-SVP FOR 6-31G(d). No justification is recorded anywhere. Both are split-valence
    double-zeta; def2-SVP is the natural partner for def2/J and RIJCOSX in ORCA, so this
    is most likely a tooling consequence of moving from Jaguar/Tinker/QoMMMa to ORCA. That
    is a reason, not a justification, and should be written down as such.

THE POINT THAT MATTERS MORE THAN EITHER, and which is stated nowhere in the project:
Claeyssens report (p. 10) that "comparison with LCCSD(T) results shows that B3LYP
underestimates the barrier to reaction somewhat, by approximately 3 kcal mol-1", and that
the underestimation is similar in enzyme and in water.

  - Their headline 11.3 kcal/mol is, on their own account, about 3 kcal/mol too low.
  - Any comparison of our absolute barrier against 11.3 inherits that and must say so.
  - The error largely CANCELS in the enzyme-minus-water difference, so the catalytic
    effect and the barrier-versus-stabilisation regression are more robust than the
    absolute number.

This materially changes how the headline barrier comparison should be worded. A barrier
of ours sitting above 11.3 is not automatically a discrepancy; 11.3 is a known
underestimate of a reference value nearer 14.

## Caveats and known issues
- convff_A_single.log is ZERO BYTES. The bridge generation script tries several argument
  forms and logs each; the one that succeeded produced an empty log. The bridge itself is
  present and is used by every later job, so the outcome is not in doubt, but there is no
  committed record of the orca_mm invocation that produced it. The script's own header
  says the AMBER file-argument mapping is ambiguous in ORCA's usage text, which is why it
  tries several forms - so which form worked is exactly the thing worth having recorded.
- The smoke test uses HF-3c and the production single point uses B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVP. The
  smoke test validates the QM/MM plumbing, not the energetics, and its energies must never
  be quoted.
- Both jobs run on a single core (%pal nprocs 1). That is fine for a single point and is
  not the production parallel setting used later.
- The single point is on frame 00820, the PILOT frame, which is in the canonical selection
  manifest but excluded from the ensemble. Appropriate for a setup validation; its energies
  are not ensemble data.
