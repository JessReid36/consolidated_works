# 10_md — Molecular dynamics: minimisation, equilibration, production

## What this step did
Minimised the solvated complex under restraint, equilibrated it through five NPT stages
that relax the restraint to zero, and ran unrestrained production. A further extension run
triples the production length. This is the step that generates the conformational ensemble
from which every QM/MM frame is later drawn.

It is also the step at which three commitments from earlier steps become answerable, and
all three are discharged here.

## Key numbers
- Minimisation: 10000 cycles, solute heavy atoms restrained at 200 kcal/mol/A^2,
  mask !(:WAT,Na+) & !@H=.
- Equilibration: five stages of 20 ps each (nstlim 10000 x dt 0.002), restraint relaxed
  200 -> 100 -> 50 -> 10 -> 0 kcal/mol/A^2.
- Production: 20 ns, unrestrained, Langevin 300 K (gamma_ln 2.0), NPT at 1 atm, SHAKE on
  bonds to hydrogen, 9 A cutoff, frames every 1 ps = 20000 frames.
- Extension: a further 40 ns, giving 60 ns total. Selection manifest runs to 59999 ps.
- System: 55680 atoms throughout.

## Acceptance threshold and verdict
Verification: 29 checks, 29 PASS, 0 FAIL. See checks/step10_verify.out.

## Literature comparison
THE PROTOCOL REPRODUCES AGBAGLO ET AL. 2024 p. 7, element for element:

    "An energy minimization of the system was first carried out with protein heavy atoms
     constrained to their crystallographic coordinates using a harmonic positional
     restraint kpos of 200 kcal mol-1/A2 ... The protein heavy atom constraints were then
     iteratively relaxed over five 20 ps simulations using Langevin dynamics under
     constant-temperature, constant-pressure (NPT) conditions at 300 K and 1 atm; the
     SHAKE algorithm was used to constrain all bonds involving hydrogen atoms ... The
     protein was then allowed to move freely for a 20 ns production-level run. The
     timescale of each frame was 1 ps, for a total of 20,000 frames."

Every element matches, read from the committed .in files: 200 kcal/mol/A^2, five 20 ps
stages, the relaxation ladder, Langevin, 300 K, 1 atm, SHAKE, 20 ns, 1 ps per frame,
20000 frames. With the 9 A cutoff and the ff14SB/GAFF/TIP3P build of step 09, the whole
MD pipeline is a reproduction of their protocol.

ONE REAL DEPARTURE: step10d extends production by a further 40 ns, to 60 ns total, and 29
of the 44 ensemble frames come from beyond the 20 ns the reference covers. Recorded as
protocol_deviations.txt deviation 6. It is an improvement, not an error, but it must be
stated because it makes any sampling comparison against Agbaglo not like for like.

## THREE COMMITMENTS DISCHARGED HERE

ITEM 1, the graft joins, raised at step 02. Recomputed from the restarts:

      join          pre-tleap   post-min      npt5
      A 115->116        1.329      1.331     1.373
      B 1->2            1.329      1.320     1.314
      C 114->115        1.647      1.397     1.362

C 114-115 entered minimisation strained at 1.647 A, 0.053 A from failing the acceptance
window, and relaxed by 0.251 A. All 378 peptide links are inside the window at both
stages. After equilibration the worst link in the protein is C 39-40 at 1.411 A, a native
residue: the graft is no longer distinguishable from the rest of the chain. The commitment
step 02 made in its own header is honoured, with evidence. Item 2 is answered by the same
result.

ITEM 9, the active-site close contact, raised at step 09. B75:CYS:HG to CHA_C:H8 went
1.375 -> 2.641 -> 3.080 A. The H++ protein-only artefact relieved itself entirely, and
Cys75's protonation does not need revisiting in the presence of the substrate.

The other six tleap close contacts resolve too, and THREE OF THEM WERE NEVER CLASHES. The
A121/C70 pairs relieve to 3.3-4.2 A. The three "same residue" pairs sit at 1.737, 1.774
and 1.802 A and do not move, because they are geminal hydrogens on a single leucine methyl
carbon: 2 x 1.09 x sin(109.5/2) = 1.780 A. tleap's close-contact check does not exclude
1,3-bonded pairs, so it flags them. No minimisation would ever move them, and their
presence in the warning list says nothing about the structure.

ITEM 8, the high-penalty GAFF torsions, raised at step 08. The torsions C1-C2-O3-C4 and
C3-C2-O3-C4 were measured over 20000 production frames and compared against the 22 QM/MM
optimised reactants (-94.0 +/- 4.4 and +83.3 +/- 4.2 degrees):

      copy  CHA resid  site     C1-C2-O3-C4 delta/sd   C3-C2-O3-C4 delta/sd
        0       382      A          -20.4 / 36.7            -22.2 / 23.0
        1       383      B           -5.4 /  8.0             -3.3 /  7.9
        2       384      C          -18.7 / 63.9            -30.8 / 26.4

The canonical selection manifest shows ALL 44 ensemble frames come from CHA#2, residue
383, site B. Copies 0 and 2 contribute nothing. Copy 1 agrees with the QM/MM optimum to
5.4 and 3.3 degrees, with spreads of 8.0 and 7.9 against a QM/MM spread of 4.4 and 4.2.
The high-penalty parameters did not bias the sampled ensemble.

## Caveats and known issues
- CIRCULAR STATISTICS ARE MANDATORY FOR TORSIONS. The first pass of the item 8 test pooled
  all three substrate copies and used linear mean and standard deviation on a periodic
  quantity. Wrap-around inflated the spread and produced a spurious DIVERGENT verdict.
  Recomputed circularly the pooled distributions are unimodal (R = 0.906, 0.913) and the
  QM/MM mean falls in the second-most-populated bin of eighteen, 68 per cent of frames
  within 30 degrees. Pooling was the error, not the sampling. Any future torsion or angle
  comparison in this project must use circular statistics.
- ONLY ONE OF THE THREE SUBSTRATE COPIES IS USED. Every ensemble frame comes from site B.
  Sites A and C are simulated but never sampled for QM/MM.

  REASON, stated by the author 2026-09-28: a single site was chosen on computational cost,
  and Phase 2 examines a single-substrate reaction, so a three-site ensemble would buy
  nothing the design work can use. This is not recorded in any committed file and should
  be added to a method note.

  That reason explains why ONE site, not why THIS site. Site B is, on every committed
  measure, the best-behaved of the three:

      site   TSA fit RMSD   centre offset   min contact   Arg63' N   torsion sd
       A         0.1267          0.161          2.782       2.899    36.7 / 23.0
       B         0.0797          0.160          2.586       3.060     8.0 /  7.9
       C         0.1518          0.203          2.709       8.479    63.9 / 26.4

  Best superposition onto the crystallographic pose, smallest centre offset, tightest
  conformational sampling, Arg63' bound rather than swung. Site C is worst on all of them.

  THIS CUTS BOTH WAYS AND THE WRITE-UP MUST SAY SO.
  In favour: site B is the most ordered and most crystallographically faithful of the
  three, so it is a defensible representative, and a site with a swung Arg63' (C) would
  have been a poor choice for a reference ensemble.
  Against: if the ensemble is drawn entirely from the best-behaved site, the barrier
  distribution describes THAT site, not a typical active site of the trimer. The spread
  in particular - which is compared against Claeyssens' path-to-path spread - is the
  spread within one well-ordered site, not across the conformational variety the trimer
  actually exhibits. Claeyssens sampled 16 paths across active sites; we sample 44 frames
  within one.

  State the reason, state that B is the best-behaved site, and state what the ensemble
  spread therefore does and does not represent. Do not let a reader discover the
  single-site restriction from the frame numbers.
- THE TRAJECTORIES ARE NOT IN THE REPOSITORY. prod.nc and prod_ext.nc are held outside git
  under the large-file policy. Every structural check here runs from the restart files,
  which are committed. The torsion test of item 8 had to be run on the HPC.
- THE SOLVATED PDB SATURATES AT 9999 RESIDUES, so a residue-keyed index of it collapses
  waters beyond that point. step09's own header warns of this. Every atom used here sits
  in residues 1-384, far below the wrap, so the index is sound for this purpose, but the
  PDB must never be used for water or ion counts.
- NO AS-RUN SCRIPT ARCHIVE EXISTS FOR STEP 10, as for steps 08 and 09.
