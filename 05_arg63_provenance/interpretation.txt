# 05_arg63_provenance — Arg63' rotamer provenance and repair invariance

## What this step did
Asked whether the swung-away Arg63' at placed site C is inherited from the crystal or
introduced by the repair and placement workflow. step05 surveys all twelve analogue sites
in raw 2CHT, measuring the nearest Arg63 guanidinium nitrogen across all chains, and
repeats the measurement on the three placed substrates. step05b compares the repaired
A/B/C Arg63 side chains directly against raw 2CHT.

## Key numbers
- Raw 2CHT, nearest Arg63 guanidinium N per analogue site, flagged SWUNG above 4.5 A:
    A203 2.900   B201 2.876   C202 8.385 SWUNG   D206 4.323   E204 3.144
    F205 5.698 SWUNG   G209 4.034   H207 7.057 SWUNG   I208 5.480 SWUNG
    J212 5.705 SWUNG   K210 6.751 SWUNG   L211 7.282 SWUNG
  7 of 12 swung, 5 of 12 bound (42%).
- Placed complex: A 2.899, B 3.060, C 8.479 SWUNG.
- step05b: Arg63 side-chain RMSD against raw 2CHT is 0.000 A over 7 side-chain atoms in
  each of the three chains; verdict unchanged for all three.
- All twelve raw distances and all three placed distances reproduced independently to
  within 0.0005 A.

## Acceptance threshold and verdict
No gate beyond the SWUNG flag at 4.5 A, which is applied consistently everywhere.
step05b's verdict field is the substantive result: unchanged, in all three chains.
Verification: 15 checks, 15 PASS, 0 FAIL. See checks/step05_verify.out.

## THE CONCLUSION IS RIGHT BUT THE STATED ARGUMENT IS WRONG
step05_arg63_provenance.sh reasons from the SOURCE analogue site that each substrate
geometry was aligned to in step 01b - K210, J212, L211 for sites A, B, C. Its closing
lines compare raw L211 against placed C201 and conclude the asymmetry is crystallographic.

Placement transforms the SUBSTRATE into the TARGET site and does not move the protein. The
Arg63' conformation a placed substrate sees therefore belongs to its TARGET site - A203,
B201, C202 - not to its source site.

The two readings make different predictions, and the data separates them:

    lig    placed    source   src N     target   tgt N
    A       2.899      K210   6.751       A203   2.900
    B       3.060      J212   5.705       B201   2.876
    C       8.479      L211   7.282       C202   8.385

All three SOURCE sites are swung, so source-site reasoning predicts all three placed sites
swung. Observed: normal, normal, swung. Source-site reasoning is therefore FALSIFIED by
this step's own data.

Target-site reasoning predicts normal, normal, swung, and the placed distances agree with
the target-site distances to within 0.184 A.

The conclusion - the asymmetry is inherited crystallographic heterogeneity, not a workflow
artefact - stands, and is independently supported by step05b's RMSD of 0.000 A. But the
write-up must argue it from the target sites. Quoting the source-site comparison would be
quoting an argument that this step's own report disproves.

## Literature comparison
Claeyssens et al. (2011, p. 11) report that in crystal structures of BsCM (2CHT and 1COM)
Arg63 "is only bound to the substrate in ~1/3 of active sites, remaining solvent exposed
in the others". Our survey of 2CHT finds 5 of 12 bound, 42%. Same phenomenon, same
structure, consistent order.

They also cite Woodcock et al., who calculated reaction paths with and without Arg63 bound
and found little difference in barrier height at the SCCDFTB/CHARMM22 level (6.0 vs 5.7
kcal/mol), and conclude Arg63 is not important for catalysis. Claeyssens' own Table 3 gives
Arg63 a DESTABILISING average contribution of -0.6 +/- 0.5 kcal/mol.

So the site-C asymmetry is expected, documented, and by the primary comparator's own
analysis not consequential for the barrier. That is the framing to use: not a defect to
apologise for, an inherited feature with a published precedent and a published null effect.

## Caveats and known issues
- THE TWO nearest_any COLUMNS ARE NOT COMPARABLE. Raw 2CHT carries 3554 hydrogens, so its
  nearest-any-atom can be satisfied by a hydrogen on Arg63 itself (the report shows HH11,
  HE, H as closest atoms). In the placed complex the protein is heavy-atom only after step
  03 while the substrates retain their own hydrogens (24, 8 per chorismate), so the same
  column is protein-heavy against substrate-including-H. Quote the nearest_N column, which
  restricts the protein side to NE/NH1/NH2 in both cases.
- The SWUNG threshold of 4.5 A is a bare literal in the script with no stated basis. It
  separates the observed distribution cleanly here - the nearest non-swung site is 4.323 A
  and the nearest swung is 5.480 A, a gap of 1.16 A - so the classification is not
  sensitive to the exact value. Say so rather than presenting 4.5 as principled.
- scripts_as_run/ differs for step05b only, by a variable rename (placed -> protein).
  Immaterial. step05 itself is identical.
