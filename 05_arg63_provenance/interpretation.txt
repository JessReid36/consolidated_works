# 05_arg63_provenance — Arg63' rotamer provenance across the twelve crystallographic sites

## What this step did
Established whether the swung-out Arg63' guanidinium at site C is inherited from the
crystal or introduced by the repair workflow. step05 surveys Arg63' placement across all
twelve TSA sites of raw 2CHT; step05b compares the repaired A/B/C Arg63 side chains
against raw 2CHT directly.

## Key numbers
- Raw 2CHT Arg63' guanidinium distances span 2.9-7.3 A across the 12 TSA sites;
  7 of 12 swung away. The cha_c source site was already swung in the crystal.
- Repaired vs raw side-chain RMSD: chain A 0.000, B 0.000, C 0.000 A, over 7 side-chain
  atoms each. Verdict "unchanged" for all three.
- Resulting placement contact: Arg63' at 2.899 (A), 3.060 (B), 6.380 (C) A

## Acceptance threshold and verdict
Gate: repair must not alter any Arg63 side chain. PASS - 0.000 A on all three chains.
The site-C anomaly is therefore inherited, not introduced.

## Literature comparison
Claeyssens et al. (2011) report the same crystallographic heterogeneity independently,
noting that Arg63 is bound to the substrate in roughly one third of active sites and
solvent exposed in the rest, across 2CHT and 1COM. They further cite Woodcock et al.
finding little barrier difference with Arg63 bound vs unbound (6.0 vs 5.7 kcal/mol at
SCC-DFTB/CHARMM22), and conclude Arg63 is not important for catalysis. In their own
Table 3 Arg63 slightly DEstabilises the TS, at -0.6 +/- 0.5 kcal/mol.

## Caveats and known issues
- Arg63' meets the 4.0 A contact criterion at two of three sites and enters the contact
  set on the union. State this explicitly rather than presenting it as a clean 12/12.
- The expectation that site C relaxes during MD equilibration is recorded in
  step06_acceptance_decision.txt but has NOT been verified in this step. That check
  belongs to step 11 and should be cross-referenced there, not asserted here.
