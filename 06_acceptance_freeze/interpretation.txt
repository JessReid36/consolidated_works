# 06_acceptance_freeze — Acceptance freeze of the pre-protonation complex

## What this step did
Froze and checksummed the accepted pre-protonation complex (paper-frame A/B/C trimer plus
three placed chorismates) and the three ligand mol2 files, recording the acceptance
conditions and the CP2K-to-unique atom-name renaming tables.

## Key numbers
- Frozen: abc_cha_preprotonation.pdb, cha_{a,b,c}.mol2, plus renaming tables
- Protein: residues 1-127 per chain, heavy-atom only, no altlocs, no duplicate atom names
- Ligand net charge -2; TSA-fit RMSD range 0.08-0.15 A
- Checksums in sha256_step06_accepted_preprotonation.txt

## Acceptance threshold and verdict
Gate: backbone continuity intact, all active-site residues present, ligands renamed without
change to coordinates, atom types, charges or bonds. PASS.

## Literature comparison
None directly. The -2 net charge sets the QM-region charge used from step 13 onward and
matches the chorismate dianion treated by Claeyssens et al. (2011).

## Caveats and known issues
- step06_acceptance_decision.txt lists a SUPERSEDED residue set in its active-site
  architecture paragraph: Arg63', Lys60', Thr74', Cys75', Phe57', Ala59' - omitting Val73'.
  The frozen structure is unaffected; only the decision text is out of date. It should be
  corrected so it does not contradict Methods Table X.1.
- The text also asserts that site-C Arg63' is "expected to relax during MD equilibration".
  That is a prediction, not a result, and must not be quoted as one.
