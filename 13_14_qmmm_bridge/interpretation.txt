# 13_14_qmmm_bridge — QM/MM bridge and level-of-theory validation

## Why these are one step
Step 13 builds the ORCA force-field bridge from the AMBER topology and proves it runs with
a cheap method; step 14 proves the production level of theory runs on the same frame.
Neither moves an atom. Together they are one operation - establish and validate the QM/MM
partition - and splitting them would give two folders that cannot be read independently.

## What this step did
step13a converts complex_solvated.prmtop to complex_solvated.ORCAFF.prms with
orca_mm -convff -AMBER. step13b runs a HF-3c QM/MM single point on frame 00820 to prove
the bridge is usable. step14 repeats it at B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVP/def2-J/RIJCOSX, the
production level.

## Key numbers
- Bridge: complex_solvated.ORCAFF.prms, 8.6 MB, covering all 55680 atoms.
- QM region: QMAtoms {6207:6230}, 24 atoms, charge -2, multiplicity 1.
- Both jobs: SCF converged in 14 cycles, ORCA terminated normally.
- DFT single point energies:
      (MM)        -236.665890454 Eh
      bare QM     -836.786437201 Eh
      (QM/MM)   -1073.452327655 Eh
  The total is the exact sum of the other two.

## Acceptance threshold and verdict
Verification: 19 checks, 19 PASS, 0 FAIL. See checks/step13_14_verify.out.

## THE OFF-BY-ONE THAT WOULD HAVE BEEN SILENT
ORCA's QMAtoms indices are 0-based; AMBER prmtop residue pointers are 1-based. The
declared range {6207:6230} resolves to 1-based atoms 6208-6231, which is residue 383 -
CHA#2, site B, the one site the ensemble draws from. First atom C1, last atom O6, 24
atoms.

The neighbouring copies sit at 0-based 6183:6206 and 6231:6254. A single-index error in
either direction would have produced a QM region straddling two substrate copies: still 24
atoms, still charge -2, still a converging SCF, and completely wrong. Nothing in the ORCA
output would have flagged it. This mapping is now asserted by the check.

## THE THREE ENERGY SCALES, AND WHY THIS MATTERS MORE THAN IT LOOKS
A QM/MM job writes THREE lines that all begin "FINAL SINGLE POINT ENERGY":

      FINAL SINGLE POINT ENERGY (MM)        the classical subsystem
      FINAL SINGLE POINT ENERGY             the QM subsystem alone
      FINAL SINGLE POINT ENERGY (QM/MM)     the total

They differ by 236.7 Eh - about 148,000 kcal/mol. A naive grep for "FINAL SINGLE POINT
ENERGY" returns the bare QM value, not the total. Quoting the wrong one is not a rounding
error, it is a different quantity entirely, and this is exactly the confusion the project's
own discipline notes warn about (the ~-1076 Ha QM/MM scale versus the ~-836 Ha bare-
substrate scale).

BOTH HARVEST SCRIPTS GET THIS RIGHT, and that is worth recording rather than assuming:
  - step19e_harvest_barriers.py anchors on the explicit \\(QM/MM\\) form for totals and uses
    a line-end anchor for the bare form.
  - step19d_harvest.py documents all three variants in a comment before parsing them.

## Literature comparison
- QM REGION. Claeyssens et al. 2011 use a chorismate-only QM region with electrostatic
  embedding and no link atoms, because the substrate is not covalently bound to the
  protein. This work does the same: 24 atoms, the whole substrate, nothing else. That is a
  genuine match on the most consequential partition decision, and it should be stated as
  such.
- LEVEL OF THEORY. Claeyssens use B3LYP/6-31G(d). This work uses B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVP with
  def2/J and RIJCOSX. Same functional; different basis set, plus an explicit dispersion
  correction and a resolution-of-the-identity approximation that Claeyssens did not use.
  NO WRITTEN JUSTIFICATION FOR THIS EXISTS IN EITHER REPOSITORY. It is a real protocol
  deviation and it bears directly on every barrier, because basis set and dispersion both
  shift barrier heights. It needs a stated reason, and ideally a single-frame comparison
  at 6-31G(d) to quantify the offset.
  -> recorded as protocol_deviations.txt deviation 9.

## Caveats and known issues
- The bridge was built from the SOLVATED topology, which is held as a .pointer in step 09
  because it exceeds the 10 MB rule. The verification falls back to complex_dry.prmtop,
  which carries identical solute ordering because tleap appends waters and ions after the
  solute. The QM-region mapping is unaffected, but the check is one step removed from the
  file actually used.
- step13a's own header records that "the AMBER file-arg mapping is ambiguous in the usage
  text, so this tries the documented forms and reports which produces the bridge". The
  successful form is recorded in convff_A_single.log. That log is committed and should be
  cited in the methods rather than the command being presented as if it were obvious.
- Both single points use frame 00820, which is the PILOT frame excluded from the ensemble.
  That is correct for a validation job - nothing is quoted from it - but the write-up
  should not imply the DFT validation was run on an ensemble member.
- The smoke test uses HF-3c, a composite method with its own basis and corrections. It
  validates that the bridge and QM/MM machinery work; it says nothing about the production
  energetics and must not be quoted as an energy.
