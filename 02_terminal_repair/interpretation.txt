# 02_terminal_repair — Terminal repair of the 2CHT A/B/C trimer from 1DBF

## What this step did
Extracted chains A, B and C from the 2CHT deposition, globally superposed 1DBF onto that
trimer on common backbone atoms, and transferred the unresolved terminal residues from
the aligned 1DBF into the 2CHT frame. Where a grafted terminus did not meet the existing
backbone at a physical C-N distance, the terminal block was closed by minimal rigid
translation to the canonical 1.329 A. Joins outside a hard-coded permitted set are treated
as a fatal error rather than adjusted, which guards against silently moving backbone that
was never meant to move.

Consumes step 01's 2cht_raw.pdb and 1dbf_raw.pdb. Continuity is verified by SHA-256 rather
than assumed: checks/step02_verify.py check 1 confirms both inputs are byte-identical to
the files recorded in 01_inputs_acquisition/outputs/sha256_step01_inputs.txt.

## Key numbers
- Superposition: 1396 common backbone atoms, RMSD 0.8037 A. Re-derived from the two
  committed structures and reproduces the report to 4 dp.
- Grafted residues, taken from aligned 1DBF:
    chain A: 1, 116-127        (13 residues)
    chain B: 1, 115-127        (14 residues)
    chain C: 1, 2, 115-127     (15 residues)
- Peptide continuity: 378 links checked at both stages. Unadjusted 2 bad, adjusted 0 bad.
- Join adjustments, both within the permitted set:
    A 115-116: initial C-N 2.120 A, block A:116-127 shifted 0.791 A
    B 1-2:     initial C-N 2.649 A, block B:1-1    shifted 1.320 A
  Each shift equals (initial C-N - 1.329) exactly, confirming the closure is a pure
  translation to the canonical distance.
- Worst remaining link after adjustment: C 114-115 at 1.647 A, i.e. +0.318 A from
  canonical. See caveats.

## Acceptance threshold and verdict
The script's own gate: a link is bad if the C-N distance is below 1.15 A or above 1.70 A;
any bad join outside allowed_joins aborts the run. After adjustment, 0 of 378 links fall
outside that window. PASS.

checks/step02_verify.py: 19 checks, 19 PASS. Note that the check deliberately uses the
SCRIPT'S window (1.15-1.70), not a tighter tolerance. An earlier draft of the check used
+/-0.25 A around canonical and reported false failures; the check must reproduce the gate
that was actually applied, not a stricter one invented afterwards.

## Literature comparison
This resolves protocol_deviations.txt deviation 1 in our favour.

Claeyssens et al. (2011) do not mention terminal repair anywhere in their Methods. The
repair therefore has no counterpart in the primary comparator.

Agbaglo et al. (2024, doi:10.1039/d3cp06100k, p. 6) perform the same repair from the same
donor and specify their ranges per chain: "residues 1 and 116-127 from 1DBF were added to
2CHT for chain A, residues 1 and 115-127 were added for chain B, and residues 1-2 and
115-127 were added for chain C".

Our patch definition is:
    A: [1] + 116-127
    B: [1] + 115-127
    C: [1,2] + 115-127

These are IDENTICAL, chain for chain. The check verifies the grafted set in the committed
abc_repaired.pdb against both the script's hard-coded definition AND the published ranges,
so the agreement is established from the output, not from the fact that both were typed
from the same source.

So the deviation from Claeyssens is real but the choice is independently corroborated by a
published treatment of the same structure. Upgrade deviation 1 from "follows the secondary
reference" to "follows the secondary reference, ranges verified identical".

## Caveats and known issues
- THE THREE GRAFT JOINS ARE NOT EQUIVALENT. A and B were closed to 1.329 A exactly. The
  C-chain graft join at C 114-115 sits at 1.647 A and was never adjusted, because 1.647
  falls inside the acceptance window (1.15-1.70) and so was never flagged. It is the worst
  link in the final structure, 0.318 A longer than canonical. This is not a rule violation
  - it passed the gate as written - but the gate is wide, and the resulting structure has
  one stretched peptide bond going into minimisation. Worth confirming it relaxes at step
  10a rather than assuming it does.
- The A-chain closure rigidly translated TWELVE residues (A:116-127) by 0.791 A. This
  preserves internal geometry of the grafted block but displaces all of it relative to the
  crystallographic frame. The block is terminal and remote from the active site, so the
  consequence is expected to be nil, but the displacement is real and is not recorded
  anywhere except step02_join_adjustment_report.tsv.
- The superposition is global, over all 1396 common backbone atoms, not local to the graft
  regions. An 0.8037 A global RMSD means the donor geometry is not a perfect match at any
  particular terminus; local fitting would close the joins better but would distort the
  donor block. The choice made here is the simpler one and is the same one Agbaglo
  describe ("The two structures were globally aligned").
- Bond ANGLES and DIHEDRALS at the graft are not checked at this step, only the C-N
  distance. The script says so explicitly and defers them to post-minimisation. They have
  not yet been re-checked; that check belongs at step 10a and is currently outstanding.
- scripts/ and scripts_as_run/ are byte-identical for this step. (Contrast step 01, where
  they differ by a thread-limit line, and step 04, where the archive is materially stale.)
