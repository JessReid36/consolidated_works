# 02_terminal_repair — Terminal repair of the A/B/C trimer against 1DBF

## What this step did
Retained chains A, B and C of 2CHT as the paper-frame trimer and grafted the unresolved
chain termini from 1DBF after global backbone superposition. Closed out-of-tolerance
peptide links at the graft joins by minimal rigid translation to a canonical C-N distance.

## Key numbers
- Global backbone superposition RMSD 0.8037 A over 1396 atoms
- Bad peptide links before adjustment: 2; after: 0
- Peptide links checked: 378

## Acceptance threshold and verdict
Gate: zero bad peptide links after adjustment. PASS (0 of 378).

## Literature comparison
Agbaglo et al. (2024) perform the same repair with the same donor structure, adding
residues 1 and 116-127 from 1DBF for chain A, 1 and 115-127 for B, and 1-2 and 115-127
for C. Our residue-source report should be checked against that breakdown if an exact
correspondence is ever claimed - it has not been checked yet.

## Caveats and known issues
- The join closure is geometric pre-conditioning only. Graft bond angles and dihedrals
  are NOT relaxed here; they are relaxed by minimisation and equilibration in step 10 and
  must be re-checked there. Do not present the 0.8037 A RMSD as a validation of the
  graft geometry - it validates the superposition, nothing more.
