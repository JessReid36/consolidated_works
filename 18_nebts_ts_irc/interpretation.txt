# 18_nebts_ts_irc — NEB-TS band, reduced-region transition state, IRC

## What this step did
Located the transition state. A climbing-image NEB band over a large active region, then a
reduced-region OptTS with an analytical Hessian, then an IRC in both directions, plus
reduced-region optimisations of the reactant and product so that all three stationary
points sit in the SAME active region.

This step carries the enzyme barrier.

## Key numbers
All at B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVP, QM/MM electrostatic embedding, 24-atom QM region, in the
102-atom reduced active region:

      reactant   -1076.130494944 Eh
      TS         -1076.104998186 Eh
      product    -1076.151454007 Eh

      BARRIER            +16.00 kcal/mol
      reaction energy    -13.15 kcal/mol

Imaginary modes at the TS: -313.30 and -18.65 cm-1.

## THE 15.08 / 16.00 DISCREPANCY, RESOLVED BY ARITHMETIC
Both numbers circulate in the project notes. They are now reconciled from committed
energies rather than by assertion.

      16.00 = TS(102-atom region) - reactant(102-atom region)
      15.08 = TS(102-atom region) - reactant(band region)

The TS energy is identical in both, -1076.104998, so the TS is not in dispute. The entire
discrepancy is the reactant: -1076.130494944 in the reduced region against -1076.129034
in the band region, a difference of exactly 0.92 kcal/mol, which is exactly 16.00 - 15.08.

TWO ACTIVE REGIONS ARE IN PLAY AND THAT IS THE WHOLE STORY:
      NEB band active region    1959 atoms
      reduced OptTS region       102 atoms
      QM region, both             24 atoms
A QM/MM energy belongs to its active region. Differencing across regions is not a small
approximation, it is a category error, and it is what produced 15.08.

16.00 IS THE CORRECT VALUE. State the active region alongside it whenever it appears.

## THE TWO IMAGINARY MODES, AND THE PUBLISHED EXPLANATION FOR THE SMALL ONE
      -313.30 cm-1   the reaction coordinate, unambiguous
       -18.65 cm-1   magnitude 18.65, below the 50 cm-1 ghost-mode threshold

The second mode has a direct literature explanation, and it is in the paper this project
already uses for its MD protocol. Agbaglo et al. (2024), footnote 1 on p. 6:

    "our group employs the 'freeze code' scheme in Gaussian16, in which all Hessian
     elements are zero when two frozen Cartesian coordinates are involved. The phenomenon
     in which several small magnitude imaginary vibrational frequencies appear in
     thermochemical analysis does not occur in our treatment of the Hessian matrix."

That is precisely our situation: the reduced-region OptTS freezes everything outside 102
atoms, so the partial Hessian carries elements coupling frozen coordinates, and small
spurious imaginary frequencies are the known consequence. Agbaglo avoid it by zeroing
those elements; ORCA's reduced-region Hessian does not.

So the TS is a first-order saddle for all practical purposes: one genuine reaction-
coordinate mode plus one artefact of the frozen partial Hessian, with a published
attribution. Report BOTH modes, cite the threshold, cite Agbaglo's footnote, and do not
write "one imaginary mode".

## Literature comparison
- Claeyssens et al. 2011 converge NO saddle point. They take "the highest point" of an
  adiabatic mapping profile as the approximate TS, at r between -0.5 and -0.7, and say so
  openly. This work converges an actual first-order saddle with a verified imaginary mode.
  THAT IS A GENUINE METHODOLOGICAL IMPROVEMENT over the primary comparator and should be
  stated as one, not buried.
- Their enzyme barrier is 11.3 +/- 1.8 kcal/mol; ours here is 16.00 from a single frame.
  The comparison is not yet like for like: different basis set and dispersion treatment
  (deviation 9), different barrier reference state (deviation 3), and theirs is an
  ensemble mean while this is one frame. The ensemble work at step 19 is where the
  comparable quantity lives.
- The reaction energy of -13.15 kcal/mol is the exothermicity of the rearrangement, and is
  a useful sanity check that the product optimisation found the right minimum.

## THE 102-ATOM REGION IS NOT THE ENSEMBLE'S REGION, AND THE TWO MUST NOT BE CONFLATED
Added 2026-09-30 against results HEAD 372fc3d.

HANDOFF now records that the 102-atom movable region is no longer a candidate explanation
for the barrier sitting above Claeyssens, because the ENSEMBLE uses 1959 atoms including
139 mobile waters. That is correct and it does not contradict anything here. Two different
jobs with two different partitions:

      this step, single-frame reduced-region OptTS : 102 atoms movable
      the step 19 ensemble, NEB-CI bands           : 1959 atoms movable

The 16.00 kcal/mol figure belongs to the 102-atom job and to no other. The ensemble
barriers belong to the 1959-atom bands. Section 5 of the check already asserts that
energies from the two partitions must never be differenced; this is the same rule stated
from the other side. When the write-up quotes 16.00 it must name the 102-atom region, and
when it quotes an ensemble barrier it must name the 1959-atom one.

## Caveats and known issues
- The 16.00 value is from ONE frame. It is not the ensemble result and must not be
  presented as the enzyme barrier of this work without that qualification.
- The IRC forward and backward trajectories are committed as QM-region xyz files, but the
  IRC ORCA output is not, so the IRC convergence and its endpoint energies cannot be
  inspected from this repository. The IRC's role - confirming the saddle connects the
  intended reactant and product - is therefore asserted rather than verifiable here.
- The NEB band files are committed for the QM region and the active region, but not the
  band energies, so the band profile itself cannot be re-derived from this folder.
- Large files: nebts_MEP_ALL_trj.xyz (471 MB) and irc_IRC_F_trj.xyz (76 MB) are held
  outside git under the large-file policy, per CHECKSUMS_large_files.txt. What is committed
  are the QM-region subsets.
