# 04_substrate_placement — Chorismate placement and the active-site contact set

## What this step did
Placed one chorismate into each of the three sites by superposing the crystallographic
transition-state analogue of the ligand's registered SOURCE site onto the analogue of the
TARGET site, then applying that transform to the ligand. Renamed the CP2K duplicate atom
names for tleap safety. Measured protein-ligand contacts two different ways and wrote
both.

The transform is analogue-to-analogue, not ligand-to-protein: liga was aligned to the
analogue at K210, so the K210 -> A203 analogue fit is the transform that carries it into
site A. Likewise J212 -> B201 and L211 -> C202.

## CORRECTION TO AN EARLIER READING OF THIS STEP
Earlier in the audit this step was described as measuring distances for a hard-coded
residue list, with the criterion-first derivation supplied afterwards by a new script
(step04b_contact_criterion.py). THAT WAS WRONG, and the error was mine.

step04_place_chorismate_abc.sh emits TWO contact records, and they are different things:

  step04_placement_contact_report.txt   UNBIASED. Every protein heavy atom is measured
                                        against the ligand; every residue within 4.0 A is
                                        listed. No residue list is consulted. This IS a
                                        criterion-derived contact set, and it has been in
                                        the committed record all along.
  step04_catalytic_contact_report.tsv   LIST-DRIVEN. For each residue in a hard-coded set,
                                        finds the closest copy ACROSS ALL CHAINS and tests
                                        the expected same/cross chain origin. Its purpose
                                        is to test the chain-origin assignment, not to
                                        define the site.

The criterion was therefore never missing. What was missing is the cutoff SENSITIVITY and
the EXCLUSION MARGIN, neither of which step04 reports. Those are now produced by
checks/step04_verify.py section 7.

CONSEQUENCE: step04b_contact_criterion.py is REDUNDANT and should NOT be committed to the
code repo. Its unique content is the ladder and the margin, and those belong in the
verification script here, not in a new pipeline step. Withdraw it.

## Literature comparison
The twelve residues returned by the 4.0 A criterion are exactly the twelve listed by
Agbaglo et al. (2024, doi:10.1039/d3cp06100k, main text p. 8, scheme S3) - no extras, no
omissions. Because the set here is derived from this structure by a stated geometric
criterion and not adopted from the paper, the agreement is independent corroboration
rather than an inherited assumption. State it that way.

Arg116 lies outside the set at 5.501-6.834 A. Claeyssens et al. (2011) Table 3 DO report
Arg116, at 1.0 +/- 0.9 kcal/mol of electrostatic TS stabilisation, and their body text
calls it an active-site residue. The two statements are compatible: a contact criterion
selects van der Waals proximity, whereas an electrostatic contribution does not require
proximity.

## Caveats and known issues
- THE CONTACT SET IS A GEOMETRIC PLACEMENT CRITERION. It is not a QM-region definition and
  not the residue basis for the electrostatic decomposition. Do not reuse it for either.
  Excluding Arg116 from an electrostatic analysis on a distance rule would be indefensible
  in a thesis about catalysis by charges at a distance. The decomposition basis is left
  open deliberately; see the DECISION REQUIRED in writeup/methods_X1_4.md.
- Arg63' is at 6.380 A at site C against 2.899 and 3.060 A at A and B, so it meets the
  criterion at two sites of three and enters the set on the union. This is inherited
  crystallographic heterogeneity, established at step 05, not a placement artefact.
- The analogue is not isosteric with the substrate: 17 heavy atoms against 16. The
  superposition is therefore analogue-to-analogue, and the ligand rides along. It is not
  an atom-for-atom mapping of substrate onto analogue.
- The step04 script comment attributes the Agbaglo residue set to the supporting
  information; it is in the main text, p. 8. The comment also repeats the pre-correction
  wording of scheme S3. See 00_crosscutting/open_items.txt item 3.
- scripts_as_run/ is MATERIALLY STALE for this step, unlike every other step so far. The
  archived copy uses a residue list containing Arg116 and omitting Val73'. The committed
  step04_catalytic_contact_report.tsv contains Val73' and labels Arg116 second_shell, so
  the report came from the working version. Keep both copies and the divergence note.
