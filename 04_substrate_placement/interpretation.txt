# 04_substrate_placement — Chorismate placement and derivation of the contact set

## What this step did
Placed one chorismate into each of the three sites by superposing onto the crystallographic
TSA pose of its registered source site, renamed CP2K duplicate atom names for tleap safety,
and measured protein-ligand contacts against ALL chains so that same-chain vs cross-chain
origin is measured rather than assumed. step04b then derives the contact set from geometry
by a stated 4.0 A criterion instead of adopting a residue list from the literature.

## Key numbers
- TSA-fit RMSD: site A 0.1267 A, site B 0.0797 A, site C 0.1518 A
- Closest protein-ligand heavy-atom contact: A 2.782 (Arg7 NH2), B 2.586 (Tyr108 OH),
  C 2.709 (Arg7 NH2)
- Contact set at 4.0 A, n=12: Arg7, Glu78, Arg90, Tyr108, Leu115 (same chain);
  Phe57', Ala59', Lys60', Arg63', Val73', Thr74', Cys75' (adjacent chain)
- Cutoff ladder: 6 residues at 3.0 A, 12 at 3.5 A, 12 at 4.0 A, 13 at 4.5 A, 13 at 5.0 A,
  21 at 6.0 A. Stable plateau 3.5-4.0 A.
- Nearest excluded residue Ala9 at 4.449 A, so the set carries a 0.449 A margin
- Arg116 excluded at 5.501 / 6.834 / 6.396 A
- Chain-origin assignment recovered with zero mismatches in all three sites

## Acceptance threshold and verdict
Gate: every expected chain-origin assignment recovered by measurement. PASS, 12 of 12 in
all three sites.

## Literature comparison
The twelve residues returned by the 4.0 A criterion are exactly the twelve listed by
Agbaglo et al. (2024, doi:10.1039/d3cp06100k, main text p. 8, frame-selection scheme S3) -
no extras, no omissions. Because our set is derived from our own structure, this is
independent corroboration rather than an inherited assumption. Arg116 appears nowhere in
that paper as a residue.

Claeyssens et al. (2011, doi:10.1039/c0ob00691b) Table 3 DO include Arg116, at
1.0 +/- 0.9 kcal/mol electrostatic TS stabilisation, and call it an active-site residue.
The two statements are compatible: a contact criterion selects van der Waals proximity,
an electrostatic contribution does not require proximity.

## Caveats and known issues
- The contact set is a GEOMETRIC placement criterion. It is NOT a QM-region definition and
  NOT the residue basis for the electrostatic decomposition. Do not reuse it for either;
  excluding Arg116 from an electrostatic analysis on a distance rule would be indefensible
  in a thesis about catalysis by charges at a distance.
- step04b_contact_criterion.py is NOT YET COMMITTED to the code repo, and
  step04b_contact_criterion_report.tsv does not exist in the results repo. Both manifest
  entries are MISSING. Until they are committed, Methods X.1.4 Tables X.1 and X.2 cite
  files that do not exist.
- The archived step04 script uses a superseded residue list (Arg116 in, Val73' out). The
  committed contact report contains Val73' and labels Arg116 second_shell, so the report
  came from the working version and the archive is stale. Less consequential now that the
  set is derived rather than inherited.
- The step04 script comment misattributes the Agbaglo set to the supporting information;
  it is in the main text. It also repeats the pre-correction wording of scheme S3 - the
  2026 correction (doi:10.1039/d6cp90040b) restates that S2-S7 RMSDs were computed against
  the initial equilibrated MD structure, not the X-ray structure.
