Every place this work departs from Claeyssens et al. 2011, the primary comparator, with
the reason and the step where it originates. Written as each deviation is ENCOUNTERED
during the step-by-step audit, so this file is incomplete until the audit reaches step 19.

A deviation is not a fault. An UNSTATED deviation is.

Status key:

===============================================================================
DEVIATION 1 - Terminal repair follows Agbaglo, not Claeyssens
  origin step: 02_terminal_repair (inputs registered at 01)
  status: UNSTATED in the repos; CORROBORATED against Agbaglo as of 2026-09-28
===============================================================================

Claeyssens 2011 Methods (p. 2-3) describe taking 2CHT, substituting chorismate for the
bound analogue, hydrogenating and solvating. They make NO mention of unresolved termini,
of repairing them, or of a donor structure.

We graft the unresolved chain termini from PDB 1DBF. So does Agbaglo et al. 2024 (p. 6),
using the same donor, specifying residues 1 and 116-127 for chain A, 1 and 115-127 for
chain B, and 1-2 and 115-127 for chain C.

So at this step we follow the secondary reference, not the primary one.

CORROBORATION. step02_verify.py section 4 reads the donor provenance of every residue in
abc_repaired.pdb from step02_residue_source_report.tsv and compares it against both the
patch definition hard-coded in the script AND the ranges published by Agbaglo. All three
chains match Agbaglo's ranges EXACTLY - 13 grafted residues in chain A, 14 in B, 15 in C,
no extras and no omissions. The agreement is residue for residue.

This is therefore not "we followed a protocol we read about". It is a reproduction of a
published repair, verified against the publication, on a choice the primary comparator
does not report at all. State it that way.

Reason for repairing at all: leaving termini unresolved would leave unphysical chain
breaks in an MD system. Claeyssens' silence is not an argument against repair - it may
simply be unreported - but it does mean the primary comparator cannot be cited for it.

Note that step02_terminal_repair_abc.sh credits "the reference MD-preparation protocol"
without naming it. See open_items.txt item 3.

DEVIATION 2 - Substrate geometry provenance differs
  origin step: 01_inputs_acquisition
  status: UNSTATED
===============================================================================

We take three pre-aligned chorismate geometries from the CP2K examples repository at
commit d71583a411a0a7d8557e94a24b87a3ceb87ec34e, each registered by nearest centre to a
specific crystallographic analogue site (K210, J212, L211).

Claeyssens substitute chorismate for the analogue "as described in references 9 and 10"
= Ranaghan et al., Mol. Phys. 2003, 101, 2695-2714, and Org. Biomol. Chem. 2004, 2,
968-980.

Neither reference is in the reference repository and no DOI is held for either, so the
procedure being deviated from CANNOT CURRENTLY BE INSPECTED. The deviation may be large
or may be nil; that is not presently knowable. See sourcing_gaps.txt.

Note that the analogue is a different molecule from the substrate: 17 heavy atoms at site
K210 against 16 in the chorismate dianion. The superposition at step 04 is therefore not
an atom-for-atom mapping, whichever procedure is used.

===============================================================================
DEVIATION 3 - The barrier is a different quantity
  origin step: spans 15 (reactant opt) through 19 (ensemble harvest)
  status: UNSTATED
===============================================================================

Recorded here early because it affects every barrier quoted anywhere in the thesis, and
because it is currently written down nowhere in either repository.

Claeyssens 2011 computes barriers and stabilisations relative to a FIXED point on the
reaction coordinate, r = -1.8 A (RS*), not relative to the reactant minimum. The minimum
sits at r = -1.6 A in the enzyme and -2.0 A in water, so a common reference was chosen to
compare the two environments. They report the shift as worth 0.1 kcal/mol in the enzyme
and 0.8 in water. Their Fig. 2 profiles use a second definition - lowest point on the
reactant side - so two definitions coexist in that paper, 0.1 kcal/mol apart in the
enzyme.

Claeyssens 2005 uses r = -1.8 A as its reactant ENDPOINT.

Ours is a third definition: converged climbing image minus a FREELY RELAXED reactant
endpoint. We are the only one of the three that lets the reactant relax.

Direction is predictable - a relaxed reactant sits lower, so our barrier is biased HIGH
relative to theirs. The MAGNITUDE IS NOT ESTABLISHED and should not be guessed. If it is
to be quantified, the cheap test is to evaluate our reactant energy at a fixed r = -1.8 A
and difference it against the relaxed value.
