# comparator_decision.txt

Which paper each claim is measured against. Fixed before drafting; do not revisit
without recording the reason here.

## The decision

Claeyssens et al. 2011, Org. Biomol. Chem. 9, 1578-1590, doi:10.1039/c0ob00691b
  -> PRIMARY for every claim.

Claeyssens et al. 2005, Chem. Commun. 5068-5070, doi:10.1039/b508181e
  -> comparator for ONE quantity only: the enzyme-only barrier-versus-stabilisation
     regression.

Agbaglo et al. 2024, Phys. Chem. Chem. Phys. 26, 12467-12482, doi:10.1039/d3cp06100k
  (correction: PCCP 2026, 28, 8283-8284, doi:10.1039/d6cp90040b)
  -> secondary. Used for system-preparation precedent and for independent corroboration
     of residue sets. NOT a barrier comparator: it is a QM-cluster study, not QM/MM, and
     reports free energies where we report potential-energy barriers.

## Why 2005 for that one regression

2011 states its fit explicitly: "The gradient of the linear relationship fitted to both
datasets is -0.95 (R value of 0.96), and the intercept is 18.3 kcal mol-1" (p. 6). Both
datasets means the 16 enzyme paths AND the 24 water paths, 40 points combined. It is
therefore not an enzyme-only gradient and cannot be compared against an enzyme-only fit.

2005 fits 16 enzyme paths and nothing else. Its regression is the correct comparator for
an enzyme-only barrier-versus-stabilisation relation.

## Sign convention - READ BEFORE COMPARING ANY GRADIENT

The two papers use the SAME convention: stabilisation positive = stabilising, so a more
stabilised TS gives a lower barrier and the gradient is NEGATIVE.

2005's body text says only "The gradient is 0.95 and the intercept is 16.0", unsigned.
The signed fit is printed inside Fig. 2(b) and is not in the text at all:

    Y = 16.02 - 0.95 X,  R = 0.97

So both papers' gradients are -0.95 to two decimals. 2005's R = 0.97 exists only in the
figure.

OUR convention is the opposite. ensemble_barriers.tsv defines stab_TS = barrier -
barrier_vac, with negative = the environment lowers the barrier. Our gradient is
therefore the negative of theirs: our +0.978 is the same signed quantity as their -0.95.
This MUST be stated explicitly at the point of comparison or it reads as a contradiction.

## Do not do this

- Do not compare our enzyme-only gradient against 2011's -0.95. That is the combined fit.
- Do not quote 2005's 4.2 kcal/mol TS stabilisation as agreement if the claim is being
  measured against 2011, whose value is 7.3 +/- 2.0.
- Do not compare our potential-energy barriers against Agbaglo's free energies.
- Do not carry 2005 figures forward for anything except the enzyme-only regression. 2011
  supersedes it; using 2005 elsewhere needs a stated reason.
