# open_items.txt

Commitments made by one step that must be honoured at a later one, and questions raised
by the audit that cannot be closed where they arise. Each entry names the step that
raised it, the step that must close it, and what closing it requires.

An item leaves this file only when it is closed, and the entry is then moved to the
CLOSED section with the evidence.

ITEM 2 - The peptide-continuity acceptance window is wide, and one join exploits it
  raised at: 02_terminal_repair
  closes at: a decision, not a computation
  status: OPEN

step02's continuity gate flags a link as bad only if the C-N distance falls below 1.15 A
or above 1.70 A. Canonical is 1.329 A. The window is therefore roughly +/- 0.3 A.

The graft join at C 114-115 sits at 1.647 A, which is 0.318 A longer than canonical and
0.053 A inside the failure threshold. It was never flagged, never adjusted, and appears
in no report other than as part of the "0 bad links" count. It is the worst surviving
link in abc_repaired.pdb.

The two joins that WERE adjusted, at 2.120 and 2.649 A, differ from it in degree rather
than in kind. Whether C 114-115 is acceptable is a judgement the gate made implicitly by
its choice of window.

To close: either state in the write-up that the window is +/- 0.3 A and that one graft
join sits near its edge, which is the honest minimum, or tighten the gate and re-run
step 02, which is a flag under the no-rerun rule. Do not leave it implicit.
  -> Already stated in writeup/methods_X1_2.md, Inconsistencies. Keep it there.

ITEM 4 - step05's stated argument is wrong and must be corrected in the code repo
  raised at: 05_arg63_provenance
  closes at: a commit to chorismate-thesis-code
  status: OPEN

step05_arg63_provenance.sh reasons from the SOURCE analogue site each substrate geometry
was aligned to (K210, J212, L211 for sites A, B, C). Its header comment says "If the TSA
site our cha_c derives from (source L211, per step 01b) already shows the guanidinium
swung away ... the asymmetry is crystallographic", and its closing lines print a
comparison of raw L211 against placed C201.

Placement transforms the SUBSTRATE into the TARGET site and does not move the protein, so
the Arg63' conformation a placed substrate encounters belongs to its TARGET site (A203,
B201, C202). The step's own report separates the two readings:

    lig    placed    source   src N     target   tgt N
    A       2.899      K210   6.751       A203   2.900
    B       3.060      J212   5.705       B201   2.876
    C       8.479      L211   7.282       C202   8.385

All three source sites are swung, so source-site reasoning predicts all three placed sites
swung. Observed: normal, normal, swung. The header's argument is falsified by the data in
the file it writes. Target-site reasoning predicts the observed pattern and agrees to
within 0.184 A.

BLAST RADIUS IS SMALL. No committed output is wrong. step05_arg63_provenance_report.tsv is
pure data and every row reproduces to within 0.0005 A. The faulty reasoning exists only in
the header comment and in stdout, and no stdout log is committed. The conclusion the step
reaches is correct and is independently supported by step05b (side-chain RMSD 0.000 A).

This is therefore a COMMENT CORRECTION, NOT A RERUN, and does not trip the no-rerun rule.

To close: correct the header comment and the closing print in
chorismate-thesis-code/phase1_system_dev/step05_arg63_provenance.sh to compare the TARGET
site, refresh 05_arg63_provenance/scripts/ from the new commit, update the manifest pin,
and re-run checks/step05_verify.py. Check 5 already asserts the correct relation, so it
will keep passing.

Already handled and needing nothing further:
  - 05_arg63_provenance/interpretation.txt records the falsified argument.
  - checks/step05_verify.py check 5 tests both predictions mechanically.
  - The Methods X.1.4 Arg63' paragraph is written from the target sites, so the thesis
    text never repeats the bad argument.
