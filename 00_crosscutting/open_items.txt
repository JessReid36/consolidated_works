# open_items.txt

Commitments made by one step that must be honoured at a later one, and questions raised
by the audit that cannot be closed where they arise. Each entry names the step that
raised it, the step that must close it, and what closing it requires.

An item leaves this file only when it is closed, and the entry is then moved to the
CLOSED section with the evidence.

  raised at: 02_terminal_repair

  status: OPEN

step02_terminal_repair_abc.sh closes bad graft joins by rigid translation and states in

currently recorded nowhere else, and no committed output demonstrates that it was done.

Three joins are at issue, all in the script's allowed_joins set:
  A 115-116  initial C-N 2.120 A, closed by translating A:116-127 by 0.791 A
  B 1-2      initial C-N 2.649 A, closed by translating B:1-1    by 1.320 A
  C 114-115  1.647 A, NEVER FLAGGED AND NEVER ADJUSTED - see item 2

EXTENDED AT STEP 09. The tleap build surfaces seven close contacts, and SIX OF SEVEN
involve residues grafted from 1DBF: A119 and A121, clashing against native C70:TYR and

09_tleap_build/checks/step09_verify.out section 8, and record both. If the grafted blocks have relaxed toward canonical geometry,
the commitment is discharged and the write-up can say so. If they have not, the repair

ITEM 2 - The peptide-continuity acceptance window is wide, and one join exploits it
  raised at: 02_terminal_repair
  closes at: a decision, not a computation
  status: OPEN

step02's continuity gate flags a link as bad only if the C-N distance falls below 1.15 A
or above 1.70 A. Canonical is 1.329 A. The window is therefore roughly +/- 0.3 A.

The graft join at C 114-115 sits at 1.647 A, which is 0.318 A longer than canonical and
0.053 A inside the failure threshold. It was never flagged, never adjusted, and appears
in no report other than as part of the "0 bad links" count. It is the worst surviving
link in abc_repaired.pdb.

The two joins that WERE adjusted, at 2.120 and 2.649 A, differ from it in degree rather
than in kind. Whether C 114-115 is acceptable is a judgement the gate made implicitly by
its choice of window.

To close: either state in the write-up that the window is +/- 0.3 A and that one graft
join sits near its edge, which is the honest minimum, or tighten the gate and re-run
step 02, which is a flag under the no-rerun rule. Do not leave it implicit.
  -> Already stated in writeup/methods_X1_2.md, Inconsistencies. Keep it there.

ITEM 4 - step05's stated argument is wrong and must be corrected in the code repo
  raised at: 05_arg63_provenance
  closes at: a commit to chorismate-thesis-code
  status: OPEN

step05_arg63_provenance.sh reasons from the SOURCE analogue site each substrate geometry
was aligned to (K210, J212, L211 for sites A, B, C). Its header comment says "If the TSA
site our cha_c derives from (source L211, per step 01b) already shows the guanidinium
swung away ... the asymmetry is crystallographic", and its closing lines print a
comparison of raw L211 against placed C201.

Placement transforms the SUBSTRATE into the TARGET site and does not move the protein, so
the Arg63' conformation a placed substrate encounters belongs to its TARGET site (A203,
B201, C202). The step's own report separates the two readings:

    lig    placed    source   src N     target   tgt N
    A       2.899      K210   6.751       A203   2.900
    B       3.060      J212   5.705       B201   2.876
    C       8.479      L211   7.282       C202   8.385

All three source sites are swung, so source-site reasoning predicts all three placed sites
swung. Observed: normal, normal, swung. The header's argument is falsified by the data in
the file it writes. Target-site reasoning predicts the observed pattern and agrees to
within 0.184 A.

BLAST RADIUS IS SMALL. No committed output is wrong. step05_arg63_provenance_report.tsv is
pure data and every row reproduces to within 0.0005 A. The faulty reasoning exists only in
the header comment and in stdout, and no stdout log is committed. The conclusion the step
reaches is correct and is independently supported by step05b (side-chain RMSD 0.000 A).

This is therefore a COMMENT CORRECTION, NOT A RERUN, and does not trip the no-rerun rule.

To close: correct the header comment and the closing print in
chorismate-thesis-code/phase1_system_dev/step05_arg63_provenance.sh to compare the TARGET
site, refresh 05_arg63_provenance/scripts/ from the new commit, update the manifest pin,
and re-run checks/step05_verify.py. Check 5 already asserts the correct relation, so it
will keep passing.

Already handled and needing nothing further:

ITEM 8 - The two worst GAFF parameters sit on the breaking bond
  raised at: 08_ligand_gaff
  closes at: an analysis of the existing step 10 trajectories; no re-run required
  status: OPEN

parmchk2 reports 0 ATTN for cha.frcmod, and that is accurate. But ranking the substituted
parameters by penalty score gives two dihedrals at 237.0 against a next-highest of 3.0:

    c2-ce-os-c3   substituted by  X-c2-os-X   penalty 237.0
    c -ce-os-c3   substituted by  X-c2-os-X   penalty 237.0

Both span os-c3. In cha_gaff.mol2, O3 is the only atom typed os and C4 is typed c3, and
the committed step 11 analysis maps the reaction coordinate as "breaking C2-O13 -> C4-O3".
The two worst-parameterised terms in the ligand force field are therefore the dihedrals
about the bond that breaks.

SCOPE, stated carefully. This does not touch the QM/MM barrier: the substrate is in the QM
region, so the reaction is not computed with GAFF. It does touch the classical MD of step
10, which samples the reactant conformational distribution, and hence step 12, which
selects QM/MM frames from it. The near-attack geometry is dihedral-sensitive. The exposure
is a biased conformational ENSEMBLE, not a biased barrier at a given geometry.

To close: compare the C4-O3 dihedral distribution over the production trajectory against
the same dihedral in the QM/MM optimised reactants of the ensemble. Agreement would show
the parameterisation is adequate for sampling, which is all it is asked to do. This is an
analysis of trajectories that already exist and does not trip the no-rerun rule.

If it does NOT agree, the finding is not that the barriers are wrong but that the frames
were drawn from a distorted distribution, which is a sampling caveat for X.2 and the
ensemble sections.

ITEM 9 - The active-site close contact, and why it exists
  raised at: 09_tleap_build
  closes at: 10_md, restrained minimisation
  status: OPEN

tleap reports B75:CYS:HG <-> C201:CHA:H8 at 1.375 A. Cys75' is a contact-set residue,
measured at 3.188 A heavy-atom to cha_c at step 04. HG is the cysteine thiol hydrogen.

CAUSE, established: step07a submitted a protein-only structure to H++.
hpp_input_protein_only_with_TER.pdb contains 0 HETATM records. H++ placed that thiol
hydrogen with no substrate in the structure, and it landed 1.375 A from a substrate
hydrogen. This is a predictable consequence of the protein-only submission, not a defect
in H++ or in the placement.

It is the only one of the seven close contacts that touches the substrate or the active
site, and the active site is where the whole thesis lives, so it does not get the same
benefit of the doubt as a clash between two leucine methyls in a grafted tail.

To close: confirm after restrained minimisation that the Cys75 HG to substrate distance
has relaxed to a physical value. If it has not, the protonation of Cys75 specifically
needs revisiting in the presence of the substrate, which WOULD be a flag under the
no-rerun rule.
