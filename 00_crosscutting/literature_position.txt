CLAEYSSENS ET AL. 2011 - Org. Biomol. Chem. 9, 1578-1590, doi:10.1039/c0ob00691b
PRIMARY COMPARATOR
===============================================================================

System and sampling (p. 2-3)
  Starting structure: PDB 2CHT, with Bartlett's inhibitor (TS analogue) bound.
  Chorismate substituted for the analogue "as described in references 9 and 10"
    (= Ranaghan et al., Mol. Phys. 2003, 101, 2695-2714; Org. Biomol. Chem. 2004, 2,
    968-980. NEITHER IS IN THE REFERENCE REPOSITORY - see sourcing_gaps.txt).
  No terminal repair mentioned anywhere in the Methods.
  Hydrogenated and solvated, 2.8 A cut-off radius, CHARMM version of TIP3P.
  Starting points generated by SCCDFTB/CHARMM22 QM/MM MD with the chorismate QM region
    restrained to r = -0.3 A.
  Enzyme: structures from a 60 ps run after 500 ps equilibration, saved every 4 ps,
    giving 16 TS complexes.
  Water: 50 ps run, also restrained to r = -0.3 A, giving 24 paths.
  QM/MM: B3LYP, Jaguar + Tinker linked by QoMMMa.
  Reaction coordinate r = d(breaking C2-O13) - d(forming C4-C14).

Barriers and stabilisation (p. 6, p. 8)
  Enzyme QM/MM barrier          11.3 +/- 1.8 kcal/mol   (16 paths)
  Enzyme in vacuo barrier       19.2 +/- 1.3 kcal/mol
  Water in vacuo barrier        18.4 +/- 2.5 kcal/mol
  Total TS stabilisation         7.3 +/- 2.0 kcal/mol
    of which electrostatic       9.9 +/- 2.4
    of which MM                 -2.6 +/- 1.7
  TS stabilisation range in the enzyme: 11.4 down to 3.2 kcal/mol; stabilising in all
    16 cases.
  Regression, FITTED TO BOTH ENZYME AND WATER DATASETS (40 points):
    gradient -0.95, R = 0.96, intercept 18.3 kcal/mol.

Reference state - a real methodological difference (p. 6, Table 1 footnote)
  Barriers and stabilisations in the stabilisation analysis are computed relative to a
  FIXED reaction-coordinate point, r = -1.8 A (called RS*), NOT to the reactant minimum.
  The reactant minimum lies at r = -1.6 A in the enzyme and r = -2.0 A in water, so a
  common point was chosen to compare environments.
  Shifting to RS* changes the enzyme barrier by 0.1 kcal/mol and water by 0.8 kcal/mol.
  The Fig. 2 profiles are plotted relative to the lowest point on the reactant side, so
  TWO barrier definitions appear in the paper, differing by 0.1 kcal/mol in the enzyme.
  OURS IS A THIRD: converged climbing image minus a freely relaxed reactant endpoint.
  See protocol_deviations.txt, deviation 3.

TS geometry (p. 6)
  TS at r = -0.5 A.
  breaking C2-O13   2.14 +/- 0.05 A
  forming  C4-C14   2.64 +/- 0.05 A

Per-residue electrostatic TS stabilisation, Table 3 (p. 8), kcal/mol, sd in brackets
  Enzyme model total   9.9 (2.4) electrostatic,  -2.6 (1.7) MM
  Arg90    6.0 (0.8)
  Arg7     3.1 (0.5)
  Glu78    2.1 (0.3)
  Cys75    1.3 (0.3)
  Tyr108   1.1 (0.2)
  Arg116   1.0 (0.9)      <- NOTE: Arg116 IS an active-site residue for Claeyssens
  Phe57    0.8 (0.3)
  Arg63   -0.6 (0.5)      <- destabilising on average
  This is a list of computed contributions, not a definition of the active site. It omits
  Lys60, Val73, Thr74, Ala59 and Leu115, all of which are contacts in our structure.

Arg63 heterogeneity (p. 11)
  In 2CHT and 1COM, Arg63 is bound to the substrate in only ~1/3 of active sites and
  remains solvent exposed in the others. Woodcock et al. found little barrier difference
  bound vs unbound (6.0 vs 5.7 kcal/mol at SCCDFTB/CHARMM22).

Comparison to their own 2005 work (p. 12)
  "average barrier here ~11.3 kcal mol-1 and ~12.0 kcal mol-1 in previous modelling34",
  i.e. 2011 is 0.7 kcal/mol lower than 2005 at the same level of theory. Attributed to
  SCCDFTB/CHARMM22 vs AM1(PM3)/CHARMM22 starting structures: hydrogen bonds around the
  substrate broke during AM1/PM3 dynamics, notably an Arg90-Glu78 interaction forming,
  which was not seen in the SCCDFTB-derived structures.

CLAEYSSENS ET AL. 2005 - Chem. Commun. 5068-5070, doi:10.1039/b508181e
COMPARATOR FOR THE ENZYME-ONLY REGRESSION ONLY
===============================================================================

  16 enzyme paths only. No water paths.
  Two 30 ps QM/MM MD runs (AM1 and PM3), structures saved at regular intervals from
    5 to 30 ps, giving 16 TS complexes. Chorismate restrained to r = -0.3 A,
    restraint 500 kcal mol-1 A-2.
  25 A sphere, outer 5 A fixed.
  Convergence: RMS gradient below 0.1 kcal/mol/A on MM, 0.002 hartree/A on QM.
  Endpoints r = -1.8 (reactant) and r = +1.8 (product).

  Enzyme QM/MM barrier          12.0 +/- 1.7 kcal/mol
  Enzyme in vacuo barrier       16.2 +/- 0.3 kcal/mol
  TS stabilisation               4.2 kcal/mol (electrostatic term average 4.7)
  Barrier range 9 to 15 kcal/mol; 13 of 16 within 1 sd.
  Regression (enzyme only, 16 points), from Fig. 2(b), NOT from the text:
    Y = 16.02 - 0.95 X,  R = 0.97
  In all cases the TS is stabilised more than the reactant; in all but one case the
    product is destabilised.

  TS at r between -0.5 and -0.7 A. At r = -0.6 A:
    breaking C-O   2.02 +/- 0.03 A
    forming  C-C   2.63 +/- 0.03 A

  NOTE ON THE TWO PAPERS' TS GEOMETRIES: they agree on the forming bond to 0.01 A
  (2.63 vs 2.64) and DISAGREE on the breaking bond by 0.12 A (2.02 vs 2.14).

  NOTE ON IN VACUO SPREAD: 2005 sd 0.3 (30 ps sampling), 2011 sd 1.3 (60 ps). The claim
  that all in vacuo curves are near-identical weakened substantially between the two
  papers as sampling broadened.
