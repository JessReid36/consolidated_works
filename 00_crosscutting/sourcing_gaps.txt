# sourcing_gaps.txt

Citations and parameters the write-up needs that are not currently held. Each entry says
what is missing, what it blocks, and what would close it. Remove an entry when closed;
do not leave closed entries behind.

DOIs are never reconstructed from memory. Where a DOI is given below it was read from a
committed file or retrieved from a database, and the source is stated.
