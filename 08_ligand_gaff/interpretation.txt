# 08_ligand_gaff — Chorismate GAFF parameterisation

## What this step did
Derived AM1-BCC partial charges for chorismate and assigned GAFF atom types and missing
force-field parameters. The step runs on TWO MACHINES: step08a derives the charges on a
local Ubuntu workstation because the HPC AMBER22 sqm cannot run (missing libopenblas.so.0,
no BLAS module fixed it), and step08b does GAFF typing and parmchk2 on the HPC in
read-charge mode, which needs no sqm. Only the charge file crosses between them.

One canonical template (cha_a) parameterises all three substrate copies. That is licensed
by step08b's identity check: all three share atom names and bond topology exactly.

## Key numbers
- charges_am1bcc.dat: 24 values summing to exactly -2.00000000.
- Raw AM1-BCC charges as written to mol2 at 4 dp summed to -1.997000. The -0.003 residual
  was distributed EVENLY over 24 atoms, -0.000125 e each. Reproduced: per-atom shift
  spread 1.11e-16 e.
- cha_gaff.mol2 charges equal charges_am1bcc.dat to 0.00e+00 e (read-charge mode, -c rc).
- GAFF types: c, c2, c3, ce, h1, ha, ho, o, oh, os. No Tripos dot-types survive.
- frcmod: 0 ATTN, 0 MISSING. Four substituted parameters, penalties 237.0, 237.0, 3.0, 2.0.
- Three-copy identity: cha_a/b/c all 24 atoms, 24 bonds, net -2.0000, names and topology
  matching.
- Toolchain: AmberTools 19 for the charges (workstation), antechamber 22.0 for the typing
  (HPC).

## Acceptance threshold and verdict
Script gates: all inputs present and non-empty; the three copies must share names and
topology; antechamber and parmchk2 must return zero; cha_gaff.mol2 and cha.frcmod must be
non-empty. All passed.
Verification: 28 checks, 28 PASS, 0 FAIL. See checks/step08_verify.out.

## THE FINDING: two dihedral parameters at penalty 237, on the breaking bond
parmchk2 reports 0 ATTN, and the audit records that faithfully. ATTN is not the only
signal. Ranking the substituted parameters by penalty score gives:

    penalty 237.0   c2-ce-os-c3   substituted by  X-c2-os-X
    penalty 237.0   c -ce-os-c3   substituted by  X-c2-os-X
    penalty   3.0   ce-o-c-o      general improper
    penalty   2.0   c2-ce-os      substituted by  c2-ce-oh

The two high-penalty dihedrals are two orders of magnitude worse than anything else in the
file. Both span os-c3.

In cha_gaff.mol2, atom 8 (O3) is the only atom typed os and atom 9 (C4) is typed c3. The
committed step 11 analysis maps the reaction coordinate as "breaking C2-O13 -> C4-O3".

SO THE TWO WORST-PARAMETERISED TERMS IN THE LIGAND FORCE FIELD ARE THE DIHEDRALS ABOUT THE
BOND THAT BREAKS IN THE REACTION.

Why it matters, stated carefully:
  - It does NOT affect the QM/MM barrier directly. The substrate is in the QM region, so
    the reaction is not computed with GAFF.
  - It DOES affect the classical MD of step 10, which samples the reactant conformational
    distribution, and therefore step 12, which selects QM/MM frames from that
    distribution. The near-attack geometry is a dihedral-sensitive quantity.
  - The risk is therefore a biased conformational ENSEMBLE, not a biased barrier at a
    given geometry.

What would settle it: compare the C4-O3 dihedral distribution in the MD against the same
dihedral in the QM/MM optimised reactants. If they agree, the parameterisation is adequate
for sampling. Tracked as 00_crosscutting/open_items.txt item 8. This is an ANALYSIS of
existing trajectories, not a re-run.

## Literature comparison
Claeyssens et al. (2011) use CHARMM22 for the protein and the CHARMM TIP3P water model,
and do not report ligand parameterisation separately; their substrate is in the QM region
throughout. Agbaglo et al. (2024) use Antechamber and GAFF, which is the direction step 08
follows and which step08b's header cites as "the reference protocol's Antechamber + GAFF
direction".

Two deviations from that reference direction, both recorded in the script's own header:
  - AMBER22 rather than AMBER18, because AMBER18 is unavailable on the HPC.
  - GAFF selected explicitly rather than gaff2, to stay close to the reference.
Neither is a free choice; both are stated.

## Caveats and known issues
- ANTECHAMBER REWROTE FOUR BOND ORDERS. C3-O1, C3-O2, C10-O5 and C10-O6 change from 'ar'
  in the template to '1' in cha_gaff.mol2. These are the two carboxylates. Connectivity is
  unchanged, both oxygens in each pair are typed 'o' and carry equal charges, which is how
  GAFF represents carboxylate delocalisation. Benign, but it means cha_gaff.mol2 is not
  byte-comparable to the template on the bond block.
- THE CHARGE RENORMALISATION IS A REAL OPERATION, NOT A ROUNDING NOTE. The AM1-BCC charges
  as written summed to -1.997, and -0.000125 e was added to every atom to reach exactly
  -2. This is defensible and is recorded in step08a_provenance.txt, but it should be
  stated in the methods rather than left implicit, because the QM region charge of -2 is
  load-bearing from X.3 onward.
- TOOLCHAIN SPLIT IS UNTESTED. Charges come from AmberTools 19, typing from antechamber
  22.0. Both versions are recorded. No committed output tests whether the two versions
  agree on GAFF typing for this molecule. Low risk, since typing is rule-based and the
  type set is chemically unremarkable, but it is untested.
- TWO DECLARED LOGS ARE ABSENT: parmchk2_cha_gaff.log and antechamber_am1bcc.log. Both are
  run logs with no result content; the parmchk2 outcome is fully captured in cha.frcmod
  and step08b_cha_gaff_audit.tsv. Recorded so the absence is deliberate.
- NO AS-RUN SCRIPT ARCHIVE EXISTS FOR STEP 08. Neither step08a nor step08b is archived in
  results 00_admin, unlike steps 01-07. scripts_as_run/ is therefore absent for this step
  rather than empty or fabricated.
- step08a runs on a workstation, so its run environment is not the HPC and is not captured
  by any HPC provenance. step08a_provenance.txt records AmberTools 19 and the input and
  output hashes, which is the whole of the record.
