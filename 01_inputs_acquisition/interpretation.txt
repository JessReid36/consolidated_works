# 01_inputs_acquisition — Acquisition and provenance locking of raw inputs

## What this step did
Retrieved PDB 2CHT (BsCM with bound transition-state analogue, 12 chains) as the
protein scaffold and PDB 1DBF (1.30 A, same catalytic homotrimer) as the terminal-repair
template, both from the RCSB. Took three pre-aligned chorismate geometries from the
CP2K examples repository at a pinned commit. Registered each ligand to the
crystallographic TSA site it was aligned against by nearest-centre matching, and locked
all five raw inputs by SHA-256.

## Key numbers
- Retrieved 2026-07-06; CP2K examples commit d71583a411a0a7d8557e94a24b87a3ceb87ec34e
- liga.mol2 -> TSA site K210, centre offset 0.161 A
- ligb.mol2 -> TSA site J212, centre offset 0.160 A
- ligc.mol2 -> TSA site L211, centre offset 0.203 A
- Each ligand 24 atoms, 16 heavy, consistent with the chorismate dianion
- 2CHT DOI 10.1073/pnas.90.18.8600; 1DBF DOI 10.1107/S0907444900004625
  (both read from the JRNL DOI record of the raw PDB files, not from memory)

## Acceptance threshold and verdict
No numerical gate at this step. The gate is identity: all five inputs checksummed into
sha256_step01_inputs.txt. PASS - recomputed 2026-09-28 against the committed copies,
5 of 5 match.

## Literature comparison
Scaffold and repair template match those used by Agbaglo et al. (2024,
doi:10.1039/d3cp06100k), who also repair 2CHT from 1DBF. Claeyssens et al. (2011,
doi:10.1039/c0ob00691b) use 2CHT as the BsCM reference structure.

## Caveats and known issues
- Ligand geometries originate from a tutorial repository, not a primary source. The
  pinned commit plus the checksums are the only identity anchor; if that repo rewrites
  history the commit reference dies but the checksums survive.
- The archived copy of step01_download_inputs.sh differs from the code-repo copy by an
  added OMP_NUM_THREADS export. Cosmetic; no output affected.
