# 01_inputs_acquisition — Acquisition and provenance locking of raw inputs

## What this step did
Retrieved the protein scaffold (PDB 2CHT, BsCM with Bartlett's transition-state analogue
bound in each active site) and the terminal-repair donor (PDB 1DBF, the 1.30 A BsCM
catalytic homotrimer) from the RCSB. Retrieved three pre-aligned chorismate geometries
from the CP2K examples repository at a pinned commit. Registered each ligand to the
crystallographic analogue site it was aligned against, by nearest centre. Locked the
identity of all five raw inputs by SHA-256.

No structural modification happens at this step. Its whole product is identified inputs
plus a provenance record.

## Key numbers
- 2CHT: 13529 ATOM, 1815 HETATM, chains A-L. Only non-water hetero is TSA (204 atoms)
  across twelve sites: A203 B201 C202 D206 E204 F205 G209 H207 I208 J212 K210 L211.
- 1DBF: 3156 ATOM, 499 HETATM, chains A, B, C.
- Ligands liga/ligb/ligc: 24 atoms, 24 bonds, formula C10H8O6, net charge -2.0000 each.
- Registration: liga -> TSA K210 (0.161 A), ligb -> J212 (0.160 A), ligc -> L211 (0.203 A).
- Registration margin to the runner-up site: 22.11 / 22.05 / 21.98 A, i.e. 138x / 139x /
  109x the winning distance.
- Retrieved 2026-07-06T06:57:45Z; CP2K examples commit d71583a411a0a7d8557e94a24b87a3ceb87ec34e.

## Acceptance threshold and verdict
No formal gate is defined by the script itself. The check script applies these:
  - all five recorded SHA-256 values reproduce against the committed files
  - 2CHT carries twelve TSA sites and no non-water hetero other than TSA
  - each ligand is 24 atoms / 24 bonds / C10H8O6 / net charge -2
  - each registration is unambiguous against its runner-up
PASS: 20 of 20 checks. See checks/step01_verify.out.

## Literature comparison
Claeyssens et al. (2011, doi:10.1039/c0ob00691b), Methods: they start from the same
entry, "the structure of Bacillus subtilis chorismate mutase taken from the protein
databank (PDB code 2CHT), containing Bartlett's inhibitor (a TS analogue) bound in the
active site", and substitute chorismate for the analogue. Our starting point and our
substitution strategy therefore match the primary comparator.

Two deviations at this step, both real and both currently unstated in the repos:

1. TERMINAL REPAIR IS NOT CLAEYSSENS'. Their Methods make no mention of repairing chain
   termini or of a donor structure. Agbaglo et al. (2024, doi:10.1039/d3cp06100k, p. 6)
   do exactly what we do: "Missing residues in the 2CHT X-ray crystal structure were
   added from the C-terminus using PDB entry 1DBF ... residues 1 and 116-127 from 1DBF
   were added to 2CHT for chain A, residues 1 and 115-127 were added for chain B, and
   residues 1-2 and 115-127 were added for chain C". So at this step we follow Agbaglo,
   not the primary comparator. -> 00_crosscutting/protocol_deviations.txt

2. LIGAND PROVENANCE DIFFERS. We take pre-aligned CP2K example geometries registered to
   specific analogue sites. Claeyssens substituted chorismate "as described in references
   9 and 10", i.e. Ranaghan, Ridder, Szefczyk, Sokalski, Hermann and Mulholland,
   Mol. Phys. 2003, 101, 2695-2714, and Org. Biomol. Chem. 2004, 2, 968-980. Neither is
   in the reference repository and no DOI is held for either, so the procedure we are
   deviating from cannot currently be inspected. -> 00_crosscutting, and SOURCING GAP.

## Caveats and known issues
- THE SCRIPT PROMISES A CHECK IT DOES NOT PERFORM. step01's comment says 24 atoms "is
  consistent with the chorismate dianion (C10H8O6) - confirm element composition and net
  charge from the mol2 before it sets the QM-region charge". The code prints only name,
  atom count and bond count. No committed output contains the elemental or charge
  confirmation. The QM-region charge of -2 therefore rested on an unperformed check until
  checks/step01_verify.py was added. The check passes; the point is that it was absent.
- step01b takes the nearest TSA centre unconditionally (dists[0]) with no ambiguity guard.
  The assignment is in fact unambiguous by 109-139x, but that is now measured rather than
  assumed.
- scripts/ and scripts_as_run/ DIFFER for both scripts, by one added environment line
  (OMP/OPENBLAS/MKL thread limits). Immaterial to output. Recorded because this same
  archive has been found materially stale for step04.
- The TSA is a different molecule from the substrate: 17 heavy atoms at site K210 against
  16 in the chorismate dianion. Expected - it is an oxabicyclic analogue - but it means
  the superposition at step 04 is not an atom-for-atom mapping.
- step01_provenance.txt records the retrieval date but not an RCSB file version. RCSB
  entries can be re-versioned; the SHA-256 record is what actually pins identity here.
