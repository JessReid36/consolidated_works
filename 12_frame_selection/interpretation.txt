# 12_frame_selection — QM/MM frame selection

## What this step did
Selected reactant frames for QM/MM path calculations. Two passes: step12a applies a site
competence filter plus the distance clause of the near-attack definition; step12b adds
frames satisfying the FULL three-condition Hur & Bruice definition, after a check found
three of the twelve failed the angular clauses.

## THE SITE OUTCOME WAS NOT A CHOICE. IT WAS A THRESHOLD.
This corrects what was recorded at step 10, where the single-site ensemble was attributed
to computational cost and the single-substrate scope of Phase 2. Those explain the frame
COUNT. They do not explain which site, and the committed log shows the site was never
chosen at all.

step12a excludes any site competent in fewer than SITE_MIN of frames, with competence
defined as Arg90-O13 < 3.2 A AND forming C6-C1 < 3.7 A, a conjunction not a distance
alone. From step12a_selection.log, with site_min at 50 per cent:

    CHA#1 (res 382, site A):   82 / 4000 competent ( 2%)   excluded
    CHA#2 (res 383, site B): 3814 / 4000 competent (95%)   QUALIFIES
    CHA#3 (res 384, site C):  883 / 4000 competent (22%)   excluded

One site passed a pre-stated threshold and two did not. The threshold, the filter and the
occupancies are all recorded in a committed log.

THIS IS A MUCH STRONGER POSITION FOR THE WRITE-UP than "we picked one site for cost". The
correct sentence is that the selection criterion excluded sites whose substrate is only
transiently bound, and only one site met it. Write it that way.

The step 11 conclusion that selection "draws from all three sites, weighted toward CHA#2"
was therefore never achievable under the criterion step12a actually implements. See
open_items item 10.

## THE NEAR-NAC FRAMES ARE WELL HANDLED, AND WORTH SAYING SO
step11f found three of the twelve step12a frames fail the full NAC definition. step12b did
not discard them. Its reasoning, from its own header:

  - All three fail on theta2 ALONE and marginally: deviations 21.4, 24.7 and 23.0 degrees
    against a 20 degree tolerance.
  - For scale, the unreactive diequatorial conformers Hur & Bruice identify in water show
    theta2 deviations near 110 degrees. These are near-NACs, not misassigned structures.
  - Rather than discard them, three ADDITIONAL frames passing all three conditions were
    selected, giving twelve full NACs plus three near-NACs.
  - The near-NACs then become a TEST: if near-attack geometry governs the barrier, they
    should sit systematically higher.

They do. Over the original set, full_NAC barriers average 13.65 kcal/mol and near_NAC
17.34. n = 3, so this is DIRECTIONAL ONLY and must not be quoted as a result. But it is
the right way to have handled marginal frames: keep them, label them, and let them test
the hypothesis rather than quietly removing them.

## Literature comparison
- The NAC criterion is Hur & Bruice, PNAS 2003, 100, 12015, doi:10.1073/pnas.1534873100,
  held as pnas.1534873100.pdf. The full three-condition form is implemented, not the
  distance clause alone.
- Claeyssens et al. 2011 selected 16 TS complexes from a 60 ps run after 500 ps of
  equilibration, saved every 4 ps, with the substrate RESTRAINED to r = -0.3 A throughout.
  OUR SELECTION IS DIFFERENT IN KIND: unrestrained frames chosen for reactant competence
  from a 60 ns unrestrained trajectory. Theirs are restrained TS-like structures; ours are
  free reactant-state structures. This is a real methodological difference and it is not
  recorded anywhere. It bears directly on what the two barrier ENSEMBLES mean, and belongs
  in protocol_deviations.
- Agbaglo et al. 2024 selected 30 frames per scheme by RMSD windows and random draw. Our
  selection is criterion-driven rather than random, which is a different sampling design
  again.

## Caveats and known issues
- SEVEN STAGE MANIFESTS EXIST AND ONE SHARES THE CANONICAL FILENAME.
  05_qmmm/selection_manifest.tsv is canonical, 45 rows.
  05_qmmm/12_frame_selection/selection_manifest.tsv is the step12a output, 12 rows.
  Copying 12_frame_selection/*.tsv into a flat directory alongside the canonical file
  silently overwrites it. THAT HAPPENED WHILE BUILDING THIS STEP FOLDER, and was caught
  only because the check compares the manifest against the ensemble. The stage files are
  now held in outputs/stage_manifests/ with the step12a one renamed, and a README
  recording the collision.
- The *_angles.tsv stage files carry a different header and a d_form column. Any parser
  reading manifests by column position must not assume one layout.
- Frame 820 is in the canonical manifest but not in the ensemble. It is the pilot frame,
  excluded elsewhere. A reader diffing the manifest against the barrier table will find
  one unexplained row unless this is stated.
- step12a scanned the 20 ns production at stride 5, but the manifest reaches 59999 ps, so
  the later frames came from a separate pass over the extension. The two passes are not
  documented together in any single committed file.
