# 11_trajectory_analysis — Production trajectory quality control

## What this step did
Fork-independent QC of the production trajectory before QM/MM frame selection: backbone
RMSD, per-residue RMSF, reaction-coordinate tracking, catalytic-contact validation,
autocorrelation, and a near-attack-conformer test. All cpptraj-free, because cpptraj is
broken cluster-wide; a stdlib NetCDF3 reader plus numpy is used instead.

This is also where the step 12 selection criterion was set, and where the interpretation
of the three-site asymmetry was fixed.

## Key numbers, all reproduced from the committed .dat files
- 11a backbone RMSD vs production frame 0: mean 3.16 A, max 4.31 A, first 2 ns 1.98 ->
  last 2 ns 3.59, drift 1.61 A. All reproduce exactly.
- 11b RMSF, 381 residues: lower-80% mean 0.686 A. Flexibility is entirely in the C-terminal
  tails - B253 13.9, B254 12.9, B252 11.7, B251 10.4, B250 8.0, C381 7.3 A.
  Contact-set residues plus Arg116: n=13, mean 0.70 A, max 0.97 A.
- 11c reaction coordinate, per copy, means over 20000 frames:
      CHA#1 (site A)  break 1.45  form 4.15  r -2.70   min form 2.97
      CHA#2 (site B)  break 1.46  form 3.29  r -1.83   min form 2.84
      CHA#3 (site C)  break 1.45  form 3.83  r -2.38   min form 2.89
  All reproduce exactly. Breaking bond spans 1.324-1.581 A across all copies, confirming
  it is a fixed force-field bond in classical MD.

## Acceptance threshold and verdict
Verification: 15 of 17 checks pass. TWO FAIL, and both are discrepancies against the
committed document rather than errors in the data. See below. The step is verified as to
its numbers; the document needs two corrections before the write-up quotes it.

## THE NUMBER THAT IS NOT IN THE DOCUMENT AND SHOULD BE
The document says "All three sample near-attack conformations (form min 2.84-2.97 A,
below the 3.7 A NAC threshold)". That is true of the MINIMA and it hides the occupancies:

      CHA#1 (site A):   3.6% of frames with d(form) <= 3.7 A
      CHA#2 (site B):  99.0%
      CHA#3 (site C):  32.4%

A 27-fold difference between site B and site A. Site B is in near-attack distance almost
always; site A almost never. Quoting the minima alone makes three very different sites
look comparable.

THIS IS THE REAL JUSTIFICATION FOR THE SINGLE-SITE ENSEMBLE, and it is much stronger than
the one recorded at step 10. Selecting reactant-competent frames from site A would have
meant cherry-picking 3.6% of the trajectory. Site B offers a near-continuous supply. The
choice was not merely convenient, it was close to forced by the sampling.

## TWO DOCUMENTATION DISCREPANCIES
1. RMSF STATISTICS MIX TWO POPULATIONS. The document quotes "Core (lower-80%) mean 0.69 A,
   median 0.74 A". 0.686 is indeed the lower-80% mean, but 0.737 is the median of ALL 381
   residues; the lower-80% median is 0.685. Both figures are individually correct and they
   describe different populations, so they must not be quoted as one statistic.

2. "ACTIVE-SITE RESIDUES ARE NOT IN THE FLEXIBLE SET" IS LOOSE. On a strict 80th-percentile
   reading (threshold 0.91 A), Arg63 and Arg116 of chain A sit marginally above it, at 0.97
   and 0.94 A. The substantive claim is sound - all 13 are below 1.0 A while the tails reach
   13.9 A - but the better sentence is the measurement: contact-set mean RMSF 0.70 A,
   maximum 0.97 A, against tails to 13.9 A. Note also that Arg63 being the most mobile
   contact-set residue is consistent with the rotamer heterogeneity established at step 05.

## A THIRD DISCREPANCY, AND IT IS THE SERIOUS ONE
The document's closing section sets the step 12 criterion as:

    "Draws from all three sites, weighted toward CHA#2; gives a Claeyssens-style spread of
     competent conformations."

THE EXECUTION DID NOT DO THIS. The canonical selection manifest, 05_qmmm/
selection_manifest.tsv, contains 45 frames and every one is CHA#2, residue 383, site B.
Sites A and C contribute nothing.

The design document and the executed selection disagree. Given the occupancies above, the
execution is the defensible one and the document is what should change - but the write-up
must not quote a criterion that was not applied. This also undercuts conclusion 2 of the
document, which argues the site asymmetry is "a feature for Claeyssens-style multi-pathway
sampling": the feature was identified and then not used.

## Literature comparison
- RMSD comparator. Agbaglo et al. 2024, scheme S2: whole-protein backbone RMSD, mean
  2.66 A, sd 0.76. Ours is 3.16 A with 1.61 A of drift over 20 ns. Same order, ours a
  little higher and drifting.
  THE 2026 CORRECTION IS LOAD-BEARING HERE. It restates that for schemes S2-S7 the RMSD was
  measured against the INITIAL EQUILIBRATED MD STRUCTURE, not the X-ray structure as
  originally published. Our RMSD is measured against production frame 0, i.e. the
  post-equilibration structure. The two are like for like ONLY under the corrected
  reading; under the original wording they would have been measuring different things.
  The document makes the comparison without stating which reference is used.

- NAC criterion. The 3.7 A threshold is properly sourced to Hur & Bruice, PNAS 2003, 100,
  12015, doi:10.1073/pnas.1534873100, held in the reference repository as
  pnas.1534873100.pdf. step11f_nac_check.py implements their FULL three-condition
  definition - distance plus two approach angles - and records that the step-12a filter
  tested distance only. Of the 12 pilot frames, 9 satisfy the full definition; 5680, 7310
  and 15825 do not, and those three are exactly the frames labelled near_NAC in
  ensemble_barriers.tsv. The labelling is traceable and correct.

- AN INTERPRETIVE HAZARD THAT MUST NOT BE BLURRED. Using a NAC criterion to SELECT
  reactant-like frames is not the same as endorsing the NAC hypothesis as an EXPLANATION
  of catalysis. Ranaghan et al. 2004 (Org. Biomol. Chem. 2, 968-980, doi:10.1039/b313759g,
  held as B313759G.pdf, and reference 10 of Claeyssens 2011) sets out the controversy
  directly: Bruice's proposal that ~90% of the catalytic effect comes from NAC formation
  has "been criticized as being unreliable", Jorgensen's MC/FEP work suggests NAC
  conversion contributes no free energy to catalysis, and the Mulholland lineage - which
  includes Claeyssens 2011, our primary comparator - argues instead for transition-state
  stabilisation.
  So the thesis uses a Bruice criterion as a filter while benchmarking against a body of
  work that disputes Bruice's mechanistic claim. That is perfectly coherent, but it must be
  said explicitly in X.2, or a reader will take the NAC filter as a mechanistic commitment
  the rest of the thesis contradicts.

## Caveats and known issues
- The barriers of the three near_NAC frames average 17.34 kcal/mol against 13.65 for the
  ten full_NAC frames of the same set. n=3, so this is not a result. It is directionally
  what Hur & Bruice would predict, and it is a reason to report the near_NAC frames
  separately rather than pooling them silently.
- Frame 08170 is labelled "other" and carries a barrier of 0.00, the collapsed band. It is
  already on the do-not-quote list.
- The document's provenance block cites prod.nc by sha256, which is good practice and
  should be kept.
- THE DOCUMENT COVERS 20 NS OF A 60 NS TRAJECTORY. The full-range statistics have now
  been computed from the committed rmsd_vs_time_full.dat and rxn_coord_per_frame_full.dat,
  with no new simulation, and they tell a BETTER story than the 20 ns document:

    RMSD per 10 ns block:  2.65  3.67  3.89  4.03  3.83  4.03
    Mean over 60 ns 3.69 A, max 4.53 A.

  The 1.61 A "drift" the 20 ns document flagged as REVIEW is a rise to a PLATEAU. Blocks
  from 20 ns onward span only 0.20 A. The document saw the rise and not the plateau, so it
  reports as a concern something the fuller data resolves.

    Near-attack occupancy, 20 ns -> 60 ns:
      CHA#1 (site A)   3.6% ->  1.8%
      CHA#2 (site B)  99.0% -> 99.4%
      CHA#3 (site C)  32.4% -> 14.4%

  Site B holds. Sites A and C fall further away over the longer run. The ensemble draws
  29 of its 44 frames from beyond 20 ns - precisely the range where A and C are worse and
  B is unchanged. The single-site choice is therefore MORE justified over the range
  actually sampled than over the range the document analyses.
